# Brief: Test Gym Kickboxing

**This client is made up.** It exists to test a full new build, from no website to a live site that the Desk then keeps up to date. Every fact below was written by Claude on 20 Nov 2026 as a stand-in. Nothing here may be reused for a real client, and nothing is taken from one.

**Approved by Musawir, 20 Nov 2026** ("approved"), with every fact as written.

## Who the site is for
- Parents in Testville looking for a kids' class (ages 6 to 13).
- Adults looking for a beginners' fitness kickboxing class.

## The one action
**Book a free trial class.** Every page drives to it. A second action, for people not ready: message the gym on WhatsApp.

## Why now
The gym has no website. People find it on Instagram, ask in DMs about times and prices, and many never get an answer. The site has to answer "when, how much, where" without a message.

## Pages
| Page | Its one job |
|---|---|
| Home | Show what a class looks like and get the free trial booked |
| Classes and times | The weekly timetable, by age group |
| Prices | Three plans, plain, with what each includes |
| Coaches | Who teaches, and why a parent can trust them |
| Free trial | A short form: name, age group, preferred day, phone |

## The facts (made up, for his approval)
- **Address:** 12 Example Street, Testville.
- **Classes:**
  - Little Kickers, ages 6 to 8: Mondays and Wednesdays, 4:30 PM.
  - Juniors, ages 9 to 13: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 5:30 PM.
  - Adults, beginners: Mondays and Wednesdays, 7:00 PM. Saturdays, 10:00 AM.
- **Prices:** free trial class. Kids $60 a month for two classes a week. Adults $70 a month, any class. Drop-in $15.
- **Coaches:** two, "Coach A" and "Coach B". Real names, bios and photos are missing, so the site shows them as placeholders until the client sends them.
- **Phone, email, WhatsApp:** missing. Placeholders that say so.

## Brand
**Missing.** The gym has no logo, colours or photos yet. The direction sheet proposes a look, and he approves it. It must not reuse another client's look, including the HLK still.

## Photos and objects that exist
None. The shot list below goes to the client on day one. Until then, the build uses plain placeholders that are clearly marked, never stock photos of people.

## Shot list for the client
1. A kids' class in action, wide, in daylight.
2. Close-ups: gloves, pads, hand wraps, the mat.
3. Each coach, at the gym, looking at the camera.
4. The front of the building, so parents can find it.
5. The logo, if one exists, as a file.

## Who approves on the client's side
Musawir, standing in for the gym owner.

## Defaults chosen
- Framework: Astro, a fast static site that Vercel hosts well.
- The free trial form sends an email to the gym. No booking system, no payments. Those would be a new feature.
